How SilentSpeak works

Three steps, all running in under a second, entirely from your browser's camera feed.

01

Track

MediaPipe Hands extracts 21 landmark points from your hand, live, directly in the browser.

02

Translate

Landmarks stream over WebSocket to a FastAPI backend, which matches the gesture to a word.

03

Speak

The matched word is synthesized to natural speech and played back instantly in your browser.
